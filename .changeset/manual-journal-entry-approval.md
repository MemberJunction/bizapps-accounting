Manual journal entry approval (golive #353, from #320 §3). A manual journal entry (no D25 origin pair, not a batch summary) saved while its company's Accounting Company Profile has a `ManualJournalEntryApprovalThreshold` is routed for approval and raises an "Approval Request" Task in the same transaction: to the company's Approval CFO when its total debits are above the threshold, when any line is on a GL account flagged `IsControlAccount`, or when no approver role or other holder of it exists; otherwise to every active holder of `ManualJournalEntryApproverRoleID`. Nobody is ever assigned their own entry, and a CFO-level entry the CFO raised is refused with a message saying so. An entry awaiting approval is in no batch: the candidate filter leaves it out of every build, preview and sweep, an explicit selection naming it is refused, a view build drops it with a warning, and `CK_JournalEntry_ApprovedBeforeBatch` is the database floor. The batch preview reports `AwaitingApprovalCount`. Changing an entry's amounts, accounts, date or company re-routes it: the open Task is closed, any approval is cleared and a new Task is raised. New remote operation `Accounting.RecordManualJournalEntryDecision` approves (stamping `ApprovedAt` / `ApprovedByUserID`) or rejects (a reason is required); the requester may never decide, a CFO-level entry only the Approval CFO, a Standard entry a holder of the approver role or the Approval CFO.

Schema (migration `V202610181300`): `GLAccount.IsControlAccount`; `AccountingCompanyProfile.ManualJournalEntryApprovalThreshold` and `ManualJournalEntryApproverRoleID`; `JournalEntry.ApprovalLevel`, `ApprovalRequestedByUserID`, `ApprovalTaskID`, `ApprovedAt`, `ApprovedByUserID`. An empty threshold, the default, switches the feature off, so existing installs behave exactly as before.
